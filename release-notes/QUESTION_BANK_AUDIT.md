# Question-bank audit — revision release

## Scope and limitations

All 1,600 records passed structural checks: unique IDs and exact stems, four distinct answer choices, valid answer index, explanation present, and exactly 100 questions for each of 16 chapters. Randomization tests preserve correct answer text and avoid repeat concept families in short successive attempts.

This is not a complete independent academic review of all 1,600 questions. The previous bank consisted of 64 authored questions and 1,536 generated variants (four variants per concept seed). This release replaces 64 integrated variants with 64 new applied questions: four in each chapter. The remaining bank contains 1,472 generated concept variants and 128 authored/applied questions. Different wording is not necessarily a different underlying concept. Difficulty labels are editorial judgements, not calibrated difficulty statistics.

The new 64 items were checked for one intended best answer, arithmetic, appropriate distractors and explanation. Their experimental values are illustrative. They are original practice questions, not official PMDC or Cambridge examination items. No new claim of exhaustive latest-syllabus coverage is made by this release.

## Reference framework

Biological mechanisms and terminology were cross-checked against OpenStax Biology 2e sections on inheritance, enzymes, homeostasis, respiratory systems, immunity and biotechnology: https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e . Teacher review and reports remain valuable; reports are prepared locally and not sent automatically.
